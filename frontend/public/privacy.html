Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026 · Version 1.0
The short version
  • We collect your email to sign you in, and the child profile you create — age band, diet, allergens — because that's what the score is calculated against.
  • Label photos are read and then discarded. We never store the image.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or advertising trackers, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time, permanently.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the NouriScan website at nouriscan.app and the NouriScan iOS app. NouriScan is an independent project operated by its creator, not a company. Throughout this policy, "we" means that operator and "you" means the adult who created the account.

NouriScan helps a parent or caregiver read a packaged-food label faster by scoring it against the profile of a specific child. It is intended for adults. It is not designed for, marketed to, or intended to be used by children.

Your child's information

Read this part

NouriScan holds information about a child, provided by a parent or caregiver. Because the age band, diet type, and allergen list describe a child's health, we treat this as the most sensitive data in the product and handle it accordingly.

We never collect information directly from children. The account holder is an adult. Children do not sign in, create profiles, or interact with the app. NouriScan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow a child to create an account.

A real name is not required. The child's name exists only so you can tell profiles apart when you have more than one. A first name, a nickname, or a single initial all work exactly as well, and we'd encourage you to use one of those.

This information is never used for advertising or profiling. It is used for one thing: calculating the score you asked for. It is not used to build a profile, target anything at you, or train any model.

You can remove it at any time. Deleting a child profile removes it and its scan history. Deleting your account removes everything.

Photos of nutrition labels

When you photograph a label, the image is sent to our scoring service, passed to Anthropic's Claude Vision to extract the nutrition text, and then discarded. We do not save the image to our database, and we do not save it to your photo library. What we keep is the result: the product name, the nutrients that were read, and the score.

Anthropic processes the image on our behalf as a service provider and does not use it to train their models. Their handling is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Practical tip

Photograph only the label. If the packaging is in shot alongside something personal, crop it first — the same as you would with any photo you send somewhere.

We do not sell your data

We have never sold or shared personal information for money or for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have no plans to. There are no advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers in NouriScan.

We would disclose information if legally required to — a valid court order, for example — or to protect someone's safety. If that ever happened and we were permitted to tell you, we would.

How long we keep things

Your account, child profiles, and scan history are kept until you delete them. We don't currently expire data automatically, because your scan history is only useful to you if it's still there.

Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted when your account is deleted. Label photos are never stored at all.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. You'll be shown exactly what will be removed and asked to type DELETE to confirm.

This permanently removes:

  • Your account and email address
  • Every child profile you created
  • Your entire scan history
  • Any outstanding sign-in codes
There is no undo

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There's no grace period and no backup we can restore from. Because your email is removed entirely, you'd need a new invite code to sign up again.

To delete a single child profile without closing your account, use Profile → select the child → delete. You can also email us and we'll do any of this for you.

Your other rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, or object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help — we won't charge you or make it difficult. Most of it you can already see and edit directly in the app.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. We don't store passwords, because there aren't any — sign-in is a short-lived emailed code. Sign-in sessions are cryptographically signed and expire after 30 days.

No system is perfectly secure, and it would be dishonest to claim otherwise. NouriScan is a small independent project, not an enterprise with a security team. If you find a vulnerability, please email us — we'd genuinely rather hear from you than not.

This is not medical advice

NouriScan provides nutrition information for educational purposes. Scores are derived from public guidance including American Academy of Pediatrics intake references and recommended dietary allowances, applied to the profile you entered. They are not a medical assessment, and they cannot account for your child's medical history, conditions, or your doctor's specific instructions.

Always read the actual packaging for allergens before feeding your child, especially where an allergy is severe. Label data from public databases can be incomplete or out of date. Talk to your pediatrician about your child's diet.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your information — a new category of data, or a new service provider that receives it — we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
